For this second blog we already have a start point for the project. We made a decission on the way of our future work.

We both had different points of view about the features and communities that we were aiming for. Nevertheless, having a perspective into our decisions, we saw that we weren’t that confident or motivated in any of the two options, cybersecurity and/or drones, so we started thinking about other themes.

We explored the self-development communities and saw that nowadays many people are trying to change their own habits to make a noticeable change in their lives. However, there is a common problem: consistency. Hard or extreme routines end in abandonment, and that's a huge problem because we have our purpose and the will to do it, but somehow we end dropping our goals.

That is why we started talking about the possibility of making a self-development community where people can share and see each other's progress to get motivated, plus our special feature, which is going to be the one that keeps them motivated.

This feature would be based on transferring your personal stats into the virtual world and making it like you are a player in a game. Here you will see your stats like in a videogame. This feature would let you add the tasks that you are doing daily (gym, run, read, work...), and those will upgrade your level in the different aspects of your life.

Following this method, users and members of the community will see their progress weekly or monthly, and they will stay motivated to keep following their goals.

We also talked a bit about how different profiles in the app/website would look if you want a more sport-focused profile or a learning one, but we still have to ideate all the details of the app itself. The main goal here was to have the idea and work in the same way.
