Today, during class and throughout the day, I gathered and organized all the information related to the project.

I needed to create the GitHub repository and use the provided template so that everything would be ready to work on and to start changing the code in the future. Although it is still not time to modify the code, having the repository prepared will make the development process easier later on.

I also asked the professors about some doubts I had regarding the project, such as the website’s visual style and how important the main feature of the project should be.

In addition, I joined and created the team Pop Korner for my partner Andrea and me. Both of us are now correctly registered in the system.

This week, I have also started to explore how the project template works and how blog entries like the one I am writing now are structured. I think this is a very good way to maintain a clear overview of the project’s objectives and the development process in a place that can be regularly revisited. For this reason, I will try to use the blog as a tool to organize the ideas that emerge throughout the project’s development.

Finally, I started developing my ideas for the project. I have a background in Multimedia Engineering and a strong passion for drones, so I would like to create something related to drones and their community. There are many active drone communities where people share information, experiences, and content, such as Discord, Reddit, and Twitter. Because of this, I think it would be interesting to create a platform focused on this theme.

However, at this stage, I am still not sure whether these ideas would be feasible or suitable enough for this type of project. Even though I really like drones and enjoy the topic, I am also open to exploring other fields. I would like to brainstorm and develop ideas in different areas in order to find a feature that is both interesting and realistic to implement within the project’s scope. For now, I am investigating different possibilities and keeping my options open before making a final decision.

I will also discuss my ideas with my partner next week so that we can compare them and see if we reach an agreement. The goal is to develop a shared perspective on the project and come up with something that both of us enjoy and feel motivated to work on together.
